# Canonical listing packet

Use one canonical packet per physical product or sellable lot. This is the source of truth for every marketplace variant.

## Identity and evidence

- Working item name
- Brand, product family, model, variant, color, size, material, year, and identifiers when known
- Identification confidence: `high`, `medium`, or `low`
- Visible evidence: facts readable or observable in the supplied photos
- Inferences: hypotheses that still need seller confirmation
- Photo inventory and recommended display order

Never expose a full serial number, home address, receipt details, or other sensitive information in public copy. Recommend redaction or a safer crop when a photo reveals it.

## Condition and contents

- Seller-confirmed condition and platform condition label
- Tested state and the exact test performed
- Wear, defects, repairs, alterations, stains, odors, and missing parts
- Included and explicitly excluded accessories
- Quantity or lot contents

Use `untested` when operation is unknown. Avoid claims such as “works,” “authentic,” “new,” “rare,” or “complete” unless the available evidence supports them.

## Commercial terms

- Public price and currency
- Pricing rationale and comparable range
- Negotiable or fixed-price preference
- Private minimum price, if supplied; never copy this into a public field
- General item location
- Pickup and shipping options, payer, parcel size, and weight when known
- Returns or seller terms the platform permits the seller to choose

## Research record

For each consequential source record:

- page title and URL;
- access date;
- exact claim supported;
- source type: manufacturer, retailer, active marketplace listing, sold listing, or other;
- whether the comparable is truly the same model, condition, completeness, region, and approximate sale date.

Treat active asking prices and sold prices as different evidence. Do not present a search snippet, a single outlier, or a visually similar item as a reliable market value.

## Platform draft

Create one variant per target marketplace containing:

- platform and seller account, if the user has multiple accounts;
- category and subcategory;
- title within the live platform limit;
- condition choice and condition note;
- description;
- required item attributes and identifiers;
- public price, currency, and listing format;
- pickup, shipping, parcel, and return settings;
- tags or keywords when supported;
- ordered photos;
- platform-specific warnings, fees, or unresolved required fields.

Write for buyers: name the item early, make condition and included contents easy to scan, disclose defects plainly, and avoid keyword stuffing or unsupported superlatives. Platform variants may differ in length and formatting but must not contradict the canonical packet.

## Review summary

Before publication, show:

1. Identity, confidence, and any unresolved uncertainty.
2. Important defects, tested state, and included contents.
3. Research-backed price recommendation and source links.
4. Every platform's exact title, description, category, condition, public price, and fulfillment terms.
5. Any field the user still needs to decide.
