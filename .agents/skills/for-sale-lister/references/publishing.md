# Marketplace publishing controls

Read this reference whenever the workflow will open or change a seller account, create a marketplace draft, or publish a listing.

## Authorization boundary

- Browsing public product and policy pages is read-only research.
- Preparing listing copy in the conversation is not an external action.
- Creating or changing a saved marketplace draft mutates the seller account; do it only when the user asked for that draft or approved the action.
- Publishing, activating, submitting, renewing, promoting, or paying for a listing requires explicit approval of the exact material terms and named platform.
- Authorization for one product, platform, account, or version does not carry over to another.

Do not interpret a request to design, implement, demonstrate, or test the skill as authorization to touch a real marketplace account.

## Tool choice

1. Use a purpose-built connector or official API when one is available and supports the needed action.
2. Otherwise use the browser with the user's existing signed-in session.
3. If neither is available, finish the listing packet and report publication as blocked. Do not claim the listing was placed.

Never request passwords, session cookies, API secrets, recovery codes, or raw payment credentials in chat. Let the platform or browser handle authentication.

## Safe submission

Before filling a form, check the seller's active listings and drafts for a likely duplicate. Match on strong identifiers, title, and photos rather than title alone.

Before the final action, confirm that the live form still matches the approved:

- account and platform;
- product identity and photos;
- title, description, category, and condition;
- price, currency, quantity, and listing format;
- pickup, shipping, returns, and any paid promotion choice.

Do not enable paid boosts, ads, subscriptions, auto-renewal, discounts, or cross-posting unless the approval expressly includes them.

Submit once, then wait for a definite result. If the page times out, reloads unexpectedly, or returns an ambiguous error, inspect active listings, drafts, notifications, or the account's item history before considering a retry. If the outcome remains ambiguous, record `outcome_unknown` and prohibit retry until it is reconciled. Never blindly retry a final submission.

## Stop and hand control to the user

Pause when the platform requires:

- CAPTCHA or another anti-bot challenge;
- MFA, passkey, or identity verification;
- tax, legal, safety, authenticity, ownership, or policy attestation;
- payment confirmation or an unexpected fee;
- a seller-specific fact or choice absent from the approved packet;
- acceptance of materially changed copy or terms generated by the platform.

State the exact screen and required action. Resume only after the user completes it and asks to continue. Never bypass a protection or make a legal or factual attestation for the seller.

## Result record

For every target platform record:

- user-facing status in Russian: `опубликовано`, `черновик сохранён`, `заблокировано`, `ошибка`, or `результат неизвестен`;
- platform and account label;
- listing ID and URL, if available;
- time of the action;
- final public price and currency;
- warnings, fees, or follow-up actions;
- enough error detail to resume safely without repeating a successful submission.
